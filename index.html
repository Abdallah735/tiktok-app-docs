How it works

1
Login with TikTok
Connect your TikTok account securely using official Login Kit.
2
Select your media
Choose a single photo, multiple photos, or a video from your computer.
3
Add caption & privacy
Write your caption and select who can see the post.
4
Publish
Content is published to your TikTok account using the official Content Posting API.
