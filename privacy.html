Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Publisher handles information when users connect their TikTok account and use the application to publish content.

1. Information We Access

When a user authorizes the application through TikTok, the application may access the TikTok account information permitted by the scopes selected during authorization.

2. TikTok Account Information

The application may access basic TikTok profile information required to identify the connected account and provide the publishing functionality.

3. Content

Photos and videos selected by the user are processed only for the purpose of performing the publishing operation requested by the user.

4. Authentication Tokens

Authentication tokens received from TikTok may be stored locally by the application when necessary to maintain the user's authorized session.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information accessed through TikTok is used only to provide the functionality of the application and to perform actions requested by the user.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may communicate with TikTok and other services required to provide content publishing functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Deletion

Users may disconnect the application from their TikTok account and remove locally stored application data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The latest version will always be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the application developer through the contact information provided with the application.
